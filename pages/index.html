PathoMap

PathoMap is a curated host–pathogen resource for connecting bacterial pathogens, their effector proteins, and the host proteins they target. Every relationship carries stable IDs, an evidence class, and a citation, so you can see what is known, how it is supported, and what remains uncertain.

Research Objective

How do bacterial effectors connect to host proteins and pathways, and how strong is the evidence?

  • Background: Published evidence for effector–host relationships is scattered across studies and databases. Broad annotations or shared pathways can be mistaken for direct interactions.
  • Methods: Browse curated pathogens, effectors, host proteins, interactions, and references. Filter, sort, and export the tables, and inspect the provenance behind each relationship.
  • Approach: Upload your own rows to compute interaction insights and compare them with the curated dataset. Generated results are labelled by evidence class and state exactly what is missing when data are insufficient.
