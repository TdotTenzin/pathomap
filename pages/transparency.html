Limitations & Scope

What this release is (and is not), to interpret results responsibly.

  • Curated prototype, not a comprehensive resource: current tables cover a focused, curated subset (bacterial host–pathogen effectors, host proteins, and related interaction records as available in the loaded dataset).
  • Coverage is limited: interaction counts are small (demonstration-scale); absence of a record does not mean an interaction is absent in biology.
  • Evidence is labeled, not inferred silently: interaction evidence is categorized (e.g. experimental, predicted, annotation-derived, hypothetical). Labels reflect curation/provenance—not clinical or functional certainty.
  • Generated analyses are reproducible outputs: generated artifacts (e.g. interaction network outputs) depend on validated inputs and explicit methods; they are not curated ground truth.
  • Demo/sample data: built-in sample and uploaded datasets are for exploration; validate against the curated pipeline outputs for serious use.
