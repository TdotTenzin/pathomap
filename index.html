PathoMap

PathoMap is a curated host–pathogen interaction dataset. Browse pathogens, effectors, host proteins, and their interactions, along with trafficking stages and derived analyses.

Research Objective

Can the amino acid sequence features of bacterial effector proteins predict their pathogen's intracellular survival strategy?

  • Background: Intracellular pathogens evade phagolysosomal killing by arresting maturation, escaping to the cytosol, rerouting vesicles, or remodeling vacuoles; some remain extracellular.
    • Arrest phagosome maturation
    • Escape into the cytosol
    • Reroute trafficking away from lysosomes
    • Modify the vacuole into a pathogen-specific compartment
    • Persist extracellularly
  • Methods: Compute hydrophobicity, intrinsic disorder, and charge from effector sequences.
  • Approach: Train and evaluate a machine learning classifier on curated effector data to predict intracellular survival strategy.
