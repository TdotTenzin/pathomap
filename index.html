PathoMap

PathoMap is a curated host–pathogen interaction dataset. The current release focuses on the pathogen table; effector, host-protein, interaction, and trafficking tables are being rebuilt one at a time.

Research Objective

Can the amino acid sequence features of bacterial effector proteins predict their pathogen's intracellular survival strategy?

  • Background: Intracellular pathogens evade phagolysosomal killing by arresting maturation, escaping to the cytosol, rerouting vesicles, or remodeling vacuoles; some remain extracellular.
  • Methods: Compute hydrophobicity, intrinsic disorder, and charge from effector sequences; additional features include amino acid composition, molecular weight, isoelectric point (pI), aliphatic index, GRAVY, signal peptide probability, and low-complexity region count and length.
  • Approach: Train and evaluate a machine learning classifier on curated effector data to predict intracellular survival strategy.
